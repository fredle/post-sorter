# Post Sorter

Photograph a letter or bill and get what you need to do, by when, with a reminder.

Paper post still carries deadlines: bills, renewals, parking fines, school forms,
hospital appointments, council tax. Post Sorter reads the letter on your iPhone,
pulls out the sender, the action, the due date, the amount and the reference
number, and reminds you before it's due. Nothing leaves the phone.

## What it does

- **Scan or import.** Scan one or more pages with the system document camera,
  choose photos, or import a PDF or image from Files.
- **Reads the letter on the phone.** Text is recognised with Apple's Vision
  framework (accurate level, language correction on). PDFs that already contain
  text use that text directly.
- **Works out what to do.**
  - On iOS 26 with Apple Intelligence turned on, Apple's on-device Foundation
    Models framework extracts the sender, document type, summary, action, due
    date, amount and currency, and reference number.
  - Everywhere else, or if the model fails, Post Sorter uses on-device data
    detectors and patterns. It picks the due date by the words near each date
    ("pay by", "due", "before", "payment date") and ignores dates such as
    "date of issue". The app works fully without Apple Intelligence.
- **You check before saving.** After every scan a review screen shows the
  pages and every field, editable, with a date picker for the due date.
  Nothing is saved until you tap Save.
- **Reminders.** A local notification on the morning of the due date and a
  number of days before (3 by default, changeable per letter and in Settings).
  You can also add an all-day event to Calendar. Calendar access is
  write-only, so the app never reads your calendar.
- **Home screen.** *To do* is grouped into Overdue, This week and Later. Swipe
  to mark a letter done. *Filed* holds done letters and letters that need no
  action. Search covers the sender, summary, reference and the full
  recognised text.
- **Letter detail.** Full-screen pages with pinch and double-tap zoom, the
  recognised text, share as PDF, mark done or move back to To do, edit, delete.
- **Remembers senders.** When a new letter comes from a sender you've saved
  before, its type is filled in from last time.
- **Optional Face ID lock** (or Touch ID or passcode).

## Install

Post Sorter is distributed through TestFlight while in testing, and then on the
App Store. Open the TestFlight invite link on your iPhone (iOS 17 or later),
install TestFlight if asked, then tap **Install**. After release, search for
"Post Sorter" on the App Store.

## Where data lives

All data is in the app's own container on your iPhone:

- Letters (fields, recognised text, reminder settings) and remembered senders:
  a SwiftData store in the app's Application Support folder.
- Page images: JPEG files in `Application Support/Letters`, written with iOS
  data protection.
- Settings: `UserDefaults`.
- Reminders: pending local notifications held by iOS.

Deleting a letter removes its record, its page images and its reminders.
Deleting the app removes everything. The app's data is included in your normal
iPhone backup (iCloud or computer), like any other app's data. There is no
separate sync.

## Privacy

- **Nothing leaves the phone.** Post Sorter makes no network connections. There
  is no server, account, analytics, tracking or third-party SDK.
- Text recognition (Vision) and Apple Intelligence extraction (Foundation
  Models) both run on the device.
- Permissions, each asked for only when needed:
  - **Camera:** to scan letters.
  - **Notifications:** for due-date reminders, asked the first time you save a
    letter with a due date.
  - **Calendar (write-only):** only if you choose Add to Calendar.
  - **Face ID:** only if you turn on the lock.
- Photos are chosen with the system photo picker, so the app never gets access
  to your photo library.

## Building

The Xcode project is generated by XcodeGen from `ios/project.yml`, and
`.github/workflows/ios.yml` builds it on a GitHub macOS runner and uploads it to
TestFlight. Sources are in `ios/App/`. There are no third-party packages.
