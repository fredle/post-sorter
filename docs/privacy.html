Privacy policy

Post Sorter · last updated 25 November 2026
In short: Your letters stay on your iPhone. The only thing that can leave it is a feedback note you choose to send.

What the app stores

The letters you scan or import, their page images, the text read from them, the details you save (such as sender, due date, amount and reference), remembered sender names and your settings. All of it is stored only in the app's private storage on your device, and is included in your normal iPhone backups.

What leaves your iPhone

Your letters, their images and the text read from them never leave it. Text recognition and extraction happen on the device, using Apple's Vision framework and, where available, Apple's on-device Foundation Models. The only network connection Post Sorter makes is when you send a feedback note from Settings ("What would make this better?"): just the note you type and the app's version go to the developer's server at feedback.leatham.net. No name, email address or device identifier is sent, the server does not keep IP addresses, and notes are used only to improve the app and are never shared or sold. There is no account, no server, no analytics, no advertising and no tracking, and no third-party code is included.

Permissions

  • Camera: to scan letters.
  • Notifications: to remind you before a due date. Reminders are scheduled on the device.
  • Calendar (add events only): when you choose Add to Calendar. The app can't read your calendar.
  • Face ID: for the optional app lock. Face ID data is handled by iOS and never seen by the app.

Deleting your data

Delete individual letters in the app, or delete the app to remove everything it stored.

Children

The app collects no personal data from anyone, including children. A feedback note contains only what its sender types.

Changes and contact

If this policy changes, the new version will be published on this page. Questions: github.com/fredle/post-sorter/issues.
