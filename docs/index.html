Post Sorter

Photograph a letter or bill and get what you need to do, by when, with a reminder.
Need help or found a problem?
Open an issue at github.com/fredle/post-sorter/issues. Please don't include photos of real letters or personal details.

Common questions

Does Post Sorter upload my letters?
No. Text recognition and extraction run on your iPhone, and the app makes no network connections. See the privacy policy.

Do I need Apple Intelligence?
No. On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Post Sorter uses Apple's on-device model to understand the letter. On other iPhones it uses on-device text recognition and date and amount detection. Always check the details before saving.

I'm not getting reminders.
Check Settings > Notifications > Post Sorter is allowed, and that the letter has a due date. Reminders arrive on the due date and the number of days before that you chose.

How do I move my letters to a new iPhone?
Letters are stored only in the app on your device and are included in your iPhone backup (iCloud or computer). Restoring that backup on the new phone brings them back.

How do I delete everything?
Delete letters in the app, or delete the app to remove all letters, pages and reminders.
