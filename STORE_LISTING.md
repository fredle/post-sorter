## Description

Paper post still carries deadlines. Bills, insurance renewals, parking fines, school forms, hospital appointments and council tax letters arrive on paper, go on a pile, and the date slips past. That means late fees, missed appointments, or an auto-renewal at a higher price you meant to shop around.

Scanner apps capture the page and to-do apps hold the task, but nothing reads the letter and turns it into the action and the deadline. Post Sorter does, and it does it on your iPhone, because your post is private.

HOW IT WORKS
1. Scan the letter with the camera, or import a photo or PDF.
2. Post Sorter reads the text on your iPhone and fills in who it's from, what kind of letter it is, what you need to do, the due date, the amount and the reference number.
3. Check the details. Every field can be edited and nothing is saved until you tap Save.
4. Get a reminder a few days before and on the morning it's due.

WHAT YOU GET
• To do list grouped into Overdue, This week and Later
• Swipe to mark a letter done. Done letters and letters that need no action go to Filed
• Reminders on the due date and a number of days before (3 by default, set per letter)
• Add to Calendar with one tap, using write-only access so the app never reads your calendar
• Search across sender, summary, reference number and every word on the page
• Full-screen pages with zoom, the recognised text, and share as PDF
• Remembers your senders, so the next letter from the same company gets its type filled in
• Optional Face ID lock

WORKS WITH OR WITHOUT APPLE INTELLIGENCE
On iPhones with Apple Intelligence, Post Sorter uses Apple's on-device model to understand the letter. On every other iPhone it uses on-device text recognition and smart date and amount detection, and it looks for words like "pay by", "due" and "payment date" to find the right deadline. Both work fully offline.

PRIVATE BY DESIGN
Your letters never leave your iPhone. Post Sorter only goes online if you choose to send the developer a feedback note, and that note contains only what you type. There's no account, no cloud OCR, no analytics and no tracking. Letters and page images are stored only in the app on your device.

Post Sorter helps you keep track of your post. It doesn't pay bills or contact senders for you. Always check important dates against the original letter.

## App Privacy

Data collection: **Customer Support** only: the optional "What would make this better?" note from Settings, sent with the app version to the developer's server (feedback.leatham.net). Purpose: App Functionality.

- Letters, page images, recognised text, remembered senders and settings stay on the device and are never sent to the developer or anyone else.
- Linked to the user: no.
- Used for tracking: no. There are no tracking domains and no third-party SDKs. The privacy manifest (`PrivacyInfo.xcprivacy`) declares no tracking and one collected data type, customer support (not linked).
- Required-reason APIs: UserDefaults (reason CA92.1, the app's own settings).
- Permissions used, all for on-device features: Camera (scanning), Notifications (reminders), Calendar write-only (optional events), Face ID (optional lock).
